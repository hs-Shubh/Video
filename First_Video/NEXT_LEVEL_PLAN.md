# Next level plan: retention edit for the Edits + Resolve reel

## What is wrong now (measured)

A scene change detector on the current master finds **5 big visual changes in 103 s**. The longest stretch without one is **34 s** (the Edits setup, 23 s to 57 s). Every scene uses the same layout: laptop on top, host bottom left, caption at the bottom. The content is right; the picture never surprises the viewer. Viewers decide in about 1 s whether to stay, and they leave whenever nothing new has happened for about 4 s.

## The rules for version 3

1. **A new picture every 2 to 4 s.** The story and the voice stay the same; only the picture changes. That means a new shot type, a punch-in, a cut to B-roll, or the host jumping in or out.
2. **Rotate five shot types,** so no two neighbours look alike:

| Code | Shot type | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | Full screen screen recording | The app fills the frame, no laptop. Punch-ins on every click |
| **H** | Host hero | The host big and centred, kinetic words around them |
| **S** | Split | Screen on the top 60 percent, host small at the bottom reacting |
| **B** | B-roll | Real or AI footage that fills the frame (phone scrolling, desk, frustration), with 2 or 3 words on it |
| **G** | Graphic | A glass card, number, counter or icon orbit on its own, no host |

3. **Hook = result first.** The first frame is the finished reel playing full screen, not a laptop. The first 3 s hold 3 different pictures.
4. **Open loop early.** Tell people in the first 6 s that the 3 errors come at the end. That keeps them watching to the end.
5. **A progress bar** at the top (thin, saffron) and a step counter, so people see how close the end is.
6. **The host appears and disappears:** they pop in for reactions (wave, shock, thumbs up) and vanish when the screen matters.
7. **More sound for the cuts:** a soft whoosh or tick on every shot change, quiet under the voice. Still no sound on top of words.
8. **Two cuts to publish:** a **45 to 60 s main cut** for reach and the full 103 s as the "complete guide" for saves. The 30 s teaser stays.
