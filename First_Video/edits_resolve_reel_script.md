## 4. ElevenLabs: dashboard to downloaded voice file

Menu names can shift a little between updates. If a button is not where it says, look for the same word nearby.

**One-time check:** the free ElevenLabs plan allows attribution-only, non-commercial use. You said you have many credits, so if they come from a paid plan you are fine for a monetised page. If they are free-plan credits, keep the AI label on and the voice credit line in the caption.

1. Open **elevenlabs.io** and sign in. In the left sidebar click **Text to Speech**.
2. Click the text box and paste the whole content of `edits_resolve_elevenlabs_paste.txt`. Leave the blank lines. Add nothing else, because the model reads every word you paste, including notes.
3. **Pick the voice first, it matters most.** Open the voice selector at the bottom left (Your Voices). To browse more, open the **Voices** page, then the Voice Library, and filter language to Hindi or search for Indian male voices. Add 2 or 3 to My Voices. Pick one that is calm, friendly and clear, because the host speaks for about two minutes.
4. **Pick the model.** Start with **Eleven v4**. It supports Hindi and takes up to 100,000 characters in one generation. If English words sound off, try **Eleven Multilingual v2**, the most stable on long reads. Eleven v3 also works for 5,000 characters or fewer; our text is about 1,550.
5. **Voice settings** to start with: Stability 50, Similarity 75, Speed 1.0, Style exaggeration 0. Lower Stability gives more energy; higher gives a flatter read. Speed ranges from 0.7 to 1.2.
6. **Test cheaply first.** Paste only beats B01, B02 and B03 (the first three paragraphs), click **Generate**, and listen. Check that "Edits", "Resolve", "Claude", "Blackmagic" and "Apple silicon" sound right, and that the Hindi flows into the English without a break. Spend credits on the full text only after this.
7. If an English word sounds wrong, respell it in the paste file (for example in Devanagari) and test that beat again.
8. Paste the full text and click **Generate**. Generate **2 or 3 times** and keep the best take.
9. **Download.** For the best quality open the **History** tab (the panel on the right, or the history icon above Generate on a narrow screen), find the take, click its download icon and choose **WAV**. WAV, M4A and FLAC come only from History. Under Advanced you can also get MP3 192 or 256 kbps. Right after generating there is also a quick download button at the bottom right of the player.
10. Save the file as `voice/voiceover.wav` in the project folder. Keep other takes as `voiceover_take2.wav` and so on.
11. Give Claude Code three things: `voice/voiceover.wav`, the brief `CLAUDE_CODE_BRIEF_edits_resolve_reel.md`, and your screen recordings (list in the brief, section 4).

## 6. Before you record: what I could not test for you

I could not run Edits, Resolve or the plugin from here. The steps come from published guides and the tools' own pages. Please do these once before you record:

1. **Edits.** Confirm your Mac is Apple silicon (M1 or later) and Edits is the latest version. Check the Settings, Automation screen has the toggle and the Copy button for Claude Code. If Edits asks for the Claude Desktop Code tab in your setup, show that on screen too.
2. **Resolve.** Note your edition and exact version. The plugin lists 21.1.2, free or Studio, macOS only. If yours differs, tell me and I will change the Resolve lines.
3. **The both-tools demo (B11) is the part nobody has documented.** Resolve renders a file to a folder, and Edits builds the reel from that folder. Run it once end to end. If Edits will not accept the render, change B11 to two separate prompts in the same chat and I will adjust the voice line.
4. **Privacy.** The Edits Settings screen shows an Access Token next to the Copy buttons. The guide says to keep it private. Never record it unblurred; the brief has a gate that scans every frame for it.

The Resolve plugin is a small community project (MIT licence, few commits) and describes its own method as one that Blackmagic could close. Check that it still installs on the day you record.
